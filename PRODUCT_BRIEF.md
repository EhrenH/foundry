## 1. Executive summary

**Foundry** is a Cape Town-based product studio offering three services under one brand:

1. **Consulting** — fractional Product Owner / Business Analyst services for founders and dev agencies.
2. **Web** — custom websites for small-to-medium service businesses, primarily in South Africa.
3. **Referral** — a customer referral platform that lives inside client websites.

**Brand essence:** *We build things that grow.*

**Primary tagline:** *The difference between a business that exists and one that grows.*

> **⚠️ POSITIONING UPDATE (supersedes older framing below):** Foundry is no longer positioned as "a fractional consultant you book a session with." Foundry is positioned as **the key to a business's growth** — more credibility, more acquisition, more conversion. The product sold is *growth itself*, with Foundry's services (consulting, web, referral) as the *mechanisms* that deliver it. All homepage and service copy must lead with the client's **outcome** (what they become / gain), not with Foundry's **job title** (what Ehren does). See the new **Section 1.5 — Positioning & Copy Direction** immediately below for the authoritative copy guidance. Where older copy in this brief (Sections 5.x) conflicts with Section 1.5, **Section 1.5 wins.**

**Strategic logic:**
- The three services are *mechanisms of growth*, not separate product lines. Consulting sharpens what a business builds; Web gives it a presence that converts; Referral turns its customers into a growth engine. All three ladder up to one promise: growth.
- Consulting is the highest-margin, fastest-to-revenue offering and the credibility anchor.
- Web is the project-based middle layer that generates portfolio and case studies.
- Referral is the recurring-revenue SaaS-style offering with the longest-term defensibility.
- Each service is a discovery path to the others.

**Founder:** Ehren — 8 years across BA, PM, and PO roles inside a UK software agency. Cape Town-based. Comfortable across US/EU timezones.

**Build constraint:** Solo founder, no developers on payroll. All builds done by Ehren with AI coding assistance (Claude Code / Cursor). Lean stack. Free tiers wherever possible. Total monthly infrastructure cost target: under R200.

**First milestone:** Foundry's marketing site + booking system live within 2 weeks. Full referral mechanism (used by Foundry itself as live demo) live within 4 weeks.

## 1.5 Positioning & copy direction (AUTHORITATIVE — supersedes copy in Section 5)

> This section is the source of truth for *what the site says and how it's framed*. Section 5 still governs page *structure* (which sections exist, in what order), but where Section 5's example copy conflicts with the positioning or copy here, **this section wins.** Claude Code: when building or revising page copy, follow this section.

### The positioning shift

**Old framing (deprecated):** "Foundry is a fractional Product Owner / web developer / consultant. Book a session."

**New framing (authoritative):** "Foundry is the key to your business's growth. More credibility. More customers. More conversion."

Foundry does not sell a session, a job title, or hours. It sells **growth** — and the three services are the *mechanisms* through which that growth is delivered. Ehren is not "a consultant"; Ehren is the force that turns a business that merely exists into one that grows.

### The copy rules that follow from this

1. **Lead with their outcome, not Foundry's role.** Every headline and opening line names what the *client* gains or becomes — not what Ehren does for a living. "Grow faster than you could alone" not "Fractional Product Owner for hire."
2. **"You/your" outnumbers "I/we."** The copy is about the client's business and its growth, with Foundry as the mechanism. The reader should see *themselves and their growth* in the copy, with Foundry as how they get there.
3. **Name the three growth levers concretely:** more **credibility**, more **acquisition**, more **conversion**. These are the tangible outcomes. Use them.
4. **The services appear as *how*, not *what*.** Consulting, Web, and Referral are introduced as the means to the growth, framed by the outcome they produce — not as a menu of job descriptions.
5. **Keep the Apple-modern voice** (Section 2.1) — calm, declarative, short sentences, no clichés, no exclamation marks, no emojis. The *positioning* changed; the *voice* did not. Note: the word "transform" was on the old banned list but is acceptable sparingly given the growth/forge theme — still avoid "transform ative" as a cliché.
6. **The forge focal point carries the metaphor visually** (see `FOCAL_POINT_SPEC.md`): raw business refined into something valuable through heat and craft. Copy and visual tell the same story — growth as transformation.

## 2. Brand system

### 2.1 Voice

- **Apple-modern.** Calm, confident, declarative. Restraint as a statement.
- **No emojis. No exclamation marks. No corporate clichés.**
- **Banned words:** *passionate, innovative, cutting-edge, leverage, solutions, synergy, robust, seamless, world-class, best-in-class, unlock, empower, transform, revolutionize, game-changing.*
- **Preferred words:** *built, made, crafted, considered, deliberate, calm, clear, simple, work, grow, trust, real.*
- Sentences are short. Each section answers one question.

### 2.2 Visual identity

**Typeface:** Geist (via `geist` npm package, free, made by Vercel). Used for all text.

**Type scale:**
- Display (hero): 96–128px desktop, 56–72px mobile, Medium weight, -2% to -3% letter spacing
- Headline (section titles): 56–72px desktop, 40–48px mobile, Medium
- Subhead: 32–40px desktop, 24–28px mobile, Medium
- Body large: 20–24px, Regular, line-height 1.5
- Body: 17–18px, Regular, line-height 1.6
- Caption: 14px, Regular, in Foundry Stone

**Colour tokens** (use exactly these hex values):

| Token | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Foundry White | `#FFFFFF` | Primary backgrounds |
| Foundry Cream | `#FAF8F5` | Secondary section backgrounds, subtle rhythm |
| Foundry Ink | `#1A1A1A` | Headers and primary text (not pure black) |
| Foundry Stone | `#6B6B6B` | Secondary text |
| Foundry Mist | `#E8E8E8` | Borders, dividers (sparingly) |
| Foundry Ochre | `#B8860B` | Accent — CTAs, brand moments, key highlights |
| Foundry Ochre Hover | `#A07509` | Hover state for Ochre |
| Foundry Ochre Active | `#8B650A` | Active/pressed state for Ochre |

**Accent discipline:** Ochre appears on a maximum of ~5% of any screen. Reserved for primary CTAs, the brand wordmark, and intentional highlight moments. Never used as decoration.

### 2.3 Layout principles

- **Generous whitespace.** Section padding: 120–160px top/bottom on desktop, 80–100px on mobile.
- **Content max-width:** 1200px container. Text columns rarely wider than 720px.
- **Single focal point per section.** One question, one CTA.
- **Asymmetric balance** preferred over fully-centred layouts.
- **No gradients. No glassmorphism. No "modern" effects.** Restraint is the design.

### 2.4 Components

**Buttons:**
- Primary: Ochre background, white text, 16px vertical padding, 24px horizontal padding, 6px border radius, Medium weight, no icon unless functional. Hover: darken to `#A07509`.
- Secondary: Transparent background, Ink text, 1px Ink border, same padding/radius. Hover: subtle fill `#F5F5F5`.
- Text link: Ink text, no underline default, underline on hover. Never use Ochre for inline links.

**Imagery:** For v1, mostly absent. Type-led design. When images appear, they are real (real client sites, real photos) — never stock photography, never illustrated "diverse teams at laptops," never abstract gradient meshes.

**Iconography:** If needed, use Lucide (single-line, 1.5px stroke). Stick to one icon set. Icons are functional, not decorative.

**Motion:** Subtle, 300–400ms transitions, ease-out for entries, ease-in-out for transitions. No bouncy springs. No scroll-triggered animation theatrics.

## 4. Database schema (Supabase / Postgres)

### 4.1 Tables

```sql
-- Referrers: anyone who signs up to refer Foundry to others
create table referrers (
  id uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  email text unique not null,
  name text not null,
  whatsapp_number text,
  referral_code text unique not null,        -- e.g. "ehren-m" or short uuid
  source text,                                -- how they heard about Foundry
  created_at timestamptz default now(),
  updated_at timestamptz default now()
);

-- Referrals: each lead generated through a referrer's link
create table referrals (
  id uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  referrer_id uuid references referrers(id) on delete set null,
  lead_name text not null,
  lead_email text not null,
  lead_phone text,
  lead_business_name text,
  consult_type text not null,                -- founder | agency | website | referral
  message text,
  status text default 'pending',             -- pending | contacted | converted | expired
  source_path text,                          -- e.g. "/r/ehren-m"
  created_at timestamptz default now(),
  contacted_at timestamptz,
  converted_at timestamptz,
  reward_amount numeric(10,2),
  reward_paid boolean default false,
  notes text
);

-- Rewards: payout records, one per converted referral
create table rewards (
  id uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  referrer_id uuid references referrers(id) on delete restrict,
  referral_id uuid references referrals(id) on delete restrict,
  amount numeric(10,2) not null,
  status text default 'owed',                -- owed | paid
  payment_method text,                       -- eft | voucher | other
  payment_reference text,
  paid_at timestamptz,
  created_at timestamptz default now()
);

-- Bookings: calls booked via Cal.com (some via referral, some direct)
create table bookings (
  id uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  cal_booking_id text unique,                -- from Cal.com webhook
  referrer_id uuid references referrers(id) on delete set null,
  event_type text not null,                  -- founder | agency | website | referral
  scheduled_at timestamptz not null,
  attendee_name text,
  attendee_email text,
  attendee_business text,
  notes text,
  status text default 'scheduled',           -- scheduled | completed | no_show | cancelled
  created_at timestamptz default now()
);

-- Indexes
create index idx_referrers_code on referrers(referral_code);
create index idx_referrals_referrer on referrals(referrer_id);
create index idx_referrals_status on referrals(status);
create index idx_rewards_referrer on rewards(referrer_id);
create index idx_bookings_scheduled on bookings(scheduled_at);
```

### 4.2 Reward amount logic

When a referral status changes to `converted`, populate `reward_amount` based on `consult_type`:

| consult_type | reward_amount |
|---|---|
| founder | R 2,000 |
| agency | R 2,000 |
| website | R 1,500 |
| referral | R 500 |

These values should be defined as constants in `src/lib/constants.ts` so they can be updated in one place.

### 4.3 Row Level Security (RLS)

- Enable RLS on all tables.
- Anonymous users can `INSERT` into `referrals` (so the public referral form works).
- Authenticated referrers can `SELECT` their own `referrers`, `referrals`, and `rewards` rows.
- Service role (used by API routes) has full access.

### 4.4 Auth approach

For v1, **no full auth required for referrers.** Their `referral_code` acts as a soft-auth token in the URL. `/dashboard/[code]` is publicly accessible by URL but obscured by the unique code. This is acceptable for the data sensitivity level (no payments stored, no PII beyond email/phone).

Upgrade path: when rewards start flowing real money, add Supabase Auth with magic links to gate `/dashboard/[code]`.

## 6. API routes

### 6.1 `POST /api/referrers`

**Purpose:** Create a new referrer.

**Body:**
```json
{
  "name": "Sarah M",
  "email": "sarah@example.com",
  "whatsapp_number": "+27821234567",
  "source": "twitter"
}
```

**Logic:**
1. Validate fields.
2. Generate unique `referral_code` (lowercase first name + "-" + first initial of last name, deduplicated with numeric suffix if collision).
3. Insert into `referrers`.
4. Send welcome email via Resend with their dashboard link.
5. Trigger n8n webhook for any additional automation.
6. Respond with `{ referral_code }`.

### 6.2 `POST /api/referrals`

**Purpose:** Create a new referral when someone fills out the form on `/r/[code]`.

**Body:**
```json
{
  "referrer_code": "sarah-m",
  "lead_name": "Lisa K",
  "lead_email": "lisa@example.com",
  "lead_phone": "+27827654321",
  "lead_business_name": "Lisa's Salon",
  "consult_type": "referral",
  "message": "..."
}
```

**Logic:**
1. Validate fields.
2. Look up `referrer_id` from `referral_code`.
3. Insert into `referrals` with `status='pending'`.
4. Trigger n8n webhook with full payload.
5. Respond with success.

### 6.3 `POST /api/webhooks/cal`

**Purpose:** Receive booking confirmations from Cal.com.

**Logic:**
1. Verify webhook signature using `CAL_WEBHOOK_SECRET`.
2. Extract booking details.
3. If the booking came from a referral link (referrer_code in metadata), look up `referrer_id`.
4. Insert into `bookings`.
5. Trigger n8n webhook for Telegram notification.

## 7. n8n automation workflows

### 7.1 Workflow: New referral notification

**Trigger:** Webhook from `/api/referrals` (called immediately after Supabase insert).

**Steps:**
1. Lookup referrer details from Supabase (`referrers` table) using `referrer_id`.
2. Send Telegram message to Ehren:
   ```
   New referral 🟧

   Lead: {{lead_name}} ({{lead_email}})
   Phone: {{lead_phone}}
   Business: {{lead_business_name}}
   Interest: {{consult_type}}

   Referred by: {{referrer.name}} ({{referrer.email}})

   Status: pending
   ```
3. Send confirmation email to referrer via Resend:
   ```
   Subject: Thanks for referring {{lead_name}}

   Hi {{referrer.name}},

   {{lead_name}} just came through your referral link. We'll be in touch with them within 24 hours.

   You'll get another email if and when they become a Foundry client.

   — Foundry
   ```

### 7.2 Workflow: Referral converted

**Trigger:** Supabase webhook fires when a `referrals` row updates and `status` changes to `converted`.

**Steps:**
1. Calculate `reward_amount` from `consult_type` lookup table.
2. Update the `referrals` row with `reward_amount` and `converted_at`.
3. Insert a row into `rewards` with `status='owed'`.
4. Send Telegram message to Ehren:
   ```
   💰 Reward owed

   {{referrer.name}} → R{{reward_amount}}
   For: {{lead_name}} ({{consult_type}})

   Pay by EFT and mark as paid in Supabase.
   ```
5. Send email to referrer via Resend:
   ```
   Subject: {{lead_name}} just became a Foundry client

   Hi {{referrer.name}},

   {{lead_name}} signed up. Your R{{reward_amount}} reward will be paid by EFT within 7 days.

   We'll send you a confirmation when it's done.

   — Foundry
   ```

### 7.3 Workflow: Weekly summary

**Trigger:** Schedule, every Monday 09:00 SAST.

**Steps:**
1. Query Supabase for previous 7 days' activity.
2. Build summary numbers (new referrers, new referrals, converted, rewards owed, bookings).
3. Send Telegram message to Ehren:
   ```
   📊 Last week at Foundry

   New referrers: {{n_referrers}}
   New referrals: {{n_referrals}}
   Converted: {{n_converted}}
   Rewards owed: R{{rewards_owed}}
   Bookings: {{n_bookings}}
   ```

## 11. Working agreements (for Claude Code)

When working in this codebase:

- **Tailwind v4 syntax.** Use the `@theme` block in `globals.css` for tokens. Don't add a `tailwind.config.ts` unless absolutely needed.
- **Server components by default.** Only use `"use client"` when interactivity actually requires it (forms, dashboards, buttons with handlers).
- **TypeScript strict mode.** No `any` unless commented with reason.
- **No premature abstraction.** Inline first, extract to component only when used 2+ times.
- **Apple-modern discipline at every step.** If a design decision would add visual noise, cut it.
- **Match the voice in copy.** No exclamation marks, no emojis (except in n8n notifications which are internal), no banned words from section 2.1. Exception: "transform" is acceptable sparingly given the growth/forge theme. **For all page copy, follow Section 1.5 (Positioning & Copy Direction) — it is authoritative and supersedes example copy in Section 5.**
- **Test the full flow before declaring a phase done.** A referral flow that "should work" but hasn't been run end-to-end isn't done.
- **Commit often.** Every meaningful chunk of work → commit → push. Vercel auto-deploys on push to `main`.
